# Progressive Disclosure and Context Loading

Load context in this order: (1) project profile/state and mandatory rules; (2) current workflow and task contract; (3) directly relevant engine/domain docs; (4) linked decisions and evidence; (5) optional background references. Prefer canonical files and links over duplicated copied content. Record what was inspected and what remains unavailable. Context reduction must not remove constraints, permissions, acceptance criteria or unresolved risks.
