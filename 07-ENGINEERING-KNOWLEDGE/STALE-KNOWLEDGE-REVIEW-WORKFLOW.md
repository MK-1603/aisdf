# Stale Knowledge Review Workflow

1. Identify the stale entry and downstream consumers.
2. Re-check primary sources and exact versions.
3. Compare old and current behavior; record breaking changes.
4. Update the entry and linked compatibility/evaluation records.
5. Mark superseded guidance and preserve history.
6. Notify owners of affected decisions/projects.
7. Re-run relevant examples/tests and record evidence.
8. Set the next review date. If verification is impossible, retain UNVERIFIED status.

## Additional detail (carried from the starter seed)
1. Detect expired review date, deprecation notice, changed release or user challenge.
2. Mark the entry `STALE`, `SUPERSEDED`, `UNVERIFIED` or `CONFLICTED`.
3. Identify dependent prompts, decisions, adapters and projects.
4. Check authoritative current sources.
5. Compare replacement guidance and migration impact.
6. Update the canonical entry and metadata.
7. Preserve supersession links and history.
8. Notify affected workflows/maintainers.
9. Add or update validation checks.
10. Close only when dependent references are reconciled.

If authoritative verification is unavailable, leave the entry unverified and disclose the limitation.
