# Stale Knowledge Review Workflow

1. Identify the stale entry and downstream consumers.
2. Re-check primary sources and exact versions.
3. Compare old and current behavior; record breaking changes.
4. Update the entry and linked compatibility/evaluation records.
5. Mark superseded guidance and preserve history.
6. Notify owners of affected decisions/projects.
7. Re-run relevant examples/tests and record evidence.
8. Set the next review date. If verification is impossible, retain UNVERIFIED status.
