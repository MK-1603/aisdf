# Mode 1 — Problem Discovery

**Outcome:** a validated or explicitly unvalidated problem/opportunity brief and a decision to proceed, investigate, pivot or stop.

1. Capture the user's problem statement and intended outcome.
2. Identify affected users, context, frequency, severity and current alternatives.
3. Separate direct evidence from founder/user assumptions.
4. Examine feasibility, desirability, viability, timing and risks where relevant.
5. Apply inversion, premortem and pre-commit kill criteria.
6. Define scope, exclusions, success signals and cheapest next validation.
7. Record a decision: PROCEED, INVESTIGATE, PIVOT, DEFER or STOP.

Do not invent market size, customer demand or validation evidence. Venture validation is optional where the task is not a venture/product opportunity.
