# Completion Report Contract

Every report includes project/task IDs; objective; baseline revision; status; scope completed; changed artifacts; acceptance criteria and per-criterion status; commands/checks with actual results; evidence references; approvals; risks/limitations; skipped or blocked work; residual risk; branch/commit if real; deployment state if real; and recommended next action. Do not label work COMPLETE if required criteria are FAIL, NOT_RUN or BLOCKED unless an authorized waiver explicitly permits closure.

## Additional detail (carried from the starter seed)
Every major task/phase report contains:
1. Work-unit and project IDs.
2. Requested objective and approved scope.
3. Completed work and artifact paths/revisions.
4. Commands/actions actually executed.
5. Tests/checks with PASS/FAIL/NOT_RUN/BLOCKED/NOT_APPLICABLE/WAIVED.
6. Evidence references.
7. Approvals and decision records.
8. Incomplete work and blockers.
9. Risks, limitations and unverified areas.
10. Next action and receiving owner.

Never say fully completed when mandatory criteria remain unverified or incomplete.
