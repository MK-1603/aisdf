# Alternatives Evaluation Standard

For material choices, compare at least two credible options when available. Evaluate requirement fit, security, privacy, maturity, compatibility, license, cost, operational burden, ecosystem, portability, team capability and lock-in. Weight criteria before scoring; document evidence and uncertainty. State why the chosen option wins and what would trigger reconsideration. Do not create fake alternatives where constraints leave only one viable option.

## Additional detail (carried from the starter seed)
For consequential decisions, identify reasonable alternatives and compare them against the actual requirement.

## Required fields
- Decision ID and requirement.
- Candidate options and selected option.
- Official sources and verification dates.
- Functional fit and compatibility.
- Security, privacy and licensing.
- Cost and operational burden.
- Maturity, ecosystem and team skill.
- Lock-in and migration effort.
- Benefits, disadvantages and known limitations.
- Rejection rationale.
- Reconsideration triggers and approver.

Do not manufacture alternatives. If the user mandates an option or no meaningful alternative exists, record that constraint and its tradeoffs. A comparison is not complete merely because several names were listed.
