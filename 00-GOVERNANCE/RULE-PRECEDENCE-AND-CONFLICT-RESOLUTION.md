# Rule Precedence and Conflict Resolution

Apply the highest applicable authority first:
1. Applicable law, regulation and binding contractual obligations.
2. Platform/provider safety, security and access constraints.
3. Organization policies and project-owner approvals.
4. AISDF mandatory rules.
5. Approved project baseline, architecture and acceptance criteria.
6. Workflow/engine-specific instructions.
7. Individual task instructions.
8. Informal suggestions and conversational assumptions.

A lower-level instruction cannot override a higher-level constraint. If two equal-level requirements conflict, stop the affected work, record both sources, assess impact and request a decision from the designated owner. Do not choose silently.

Every exception records: rule ID, conflict, authority, decision maker, rationale, affected scope, compensating controls, residual risk, date and expiry/review date. Safety, legal and platform restrictions are not waivable by a project prompt.
